# Copy decks

The studio ships 25 ready-made posts in its sidebar. **The studio is the source of
truth** — clicking a deck loads it complete, with the right poster type, style and
accent scheme. This file is here for editing the words away from the studio, handing
them to someone else, or translating them.

**Before posting:** hero names, skin names, prices, bonus percentages, countdown times
and reviewer names are placeholders. Replace them with the real ones.

## Two conventions worth knowing

**Asterisks knock a word out.** In a Drop sheet headline, `*HUJUNG MINGGU*` sets that
phrase in a solid accent block — the loudest thing on the sheet. Give it to the word
that sells: the hero name, the price, the deadline. Consecutive marked words share one
block, so a phrase reads as a phrase.

In a **Sticker** tagline the same asterisks set a word heavier instead:
`*Top Up* Laju, *Harga* Jujur.`

**The Feedback deck takes a list.** Reviews go in one field, one per line:

```
NAME | review text | place | stars
```

The stars are optional and default to 5 — and they pick which of the five rating faces
the card shows, exactly as the site does it. The **Card** dropdown chooses which line
goes out, so keep the whole list in there and reorder it rather than retyping.

Loaded from the homepage as it stands:

```
VAVELT | dipercayai | Malaysia | 5
CEBAB | fast servis under 1 min masuk dh diamond | Malaysia | 5
AJIM | boost like luu | Malaysia | 5
FANZ | ini kali ke 10 sy boost like Tiktok kt sini | Malaysia | 5
BOLIV | Mantap | Malaysia | 5
```

## On reviews

Use real ones. A quote from a customer who did not write it is not a review, and the
five-star proof posts only work for as long as people believe them.
