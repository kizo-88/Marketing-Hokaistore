# Hokaistore: 30-day marketing action plan

Three phases, one task a day. Days that need a graphic are marked 📌 — those are the
ones the [poster studio](poster-studio/index.html) exists for, and the deck in brackets
is what to load from its sidebar. Scheduling is covered in
[posting-workflow.md](posting-workflow.md).

---

## Phase 1 · Foundation & setup (days 1–7)

**Goal:** prepare the channels and the site for incoming traffic.

- **Link-in-bio funnel.** TikTok and IG bios must state the UVP plainly:
  *"🚀 Cheapest MLBB & PUBG Top-ups | ⚡ 1-Min Delivery | 👇 Beli Sekarang"*
- **The Kaicash hook.** A promotional banner on the homepage:
  *"TOP UP RM50, GET RM55 IN KAICASH — this month only."*
- **Trust anchors.** The FEEDBACK section front and centre on the homepage.

## Phase 2 · Content & viral acquisition (days 8–21)

**Goal:** organic reach through short-form video.

**Gaming (MLBB & PUBG)**

- *The Leak* — 15 seconds of leaked art or gameplay for an upcoming skin, trending
  phonk audio, text on screen, and the CTA: *"Start saving your diamonds. Cheapest at
  Hokaistore, link in bio."*
- *The Comparison* — split screen, official store price against ours, ending with a live
  screen recording of a checkout under 60 seconds.

**Socmed services**

- *The Transformation* — "Day 1 vs day 30 of taking my brand seriously", an account
  going from 100 to 10k. CTA: *"Need a boost for brand deals? Link in bio."*

**Digital products**

- *The Cheat Code* — a common problem solved in 30 seconds by one of the templates.

**The pitch, verbatim:**

> Hey [name], love your streams. We run Hokaistore and want to sponsor you — RM100
> Kaicash monthly to top up on stream, plus a 5% affiliate code for your viewers.
> Interested?

Streamers with 50–200 average live viewers are the target. When they top up live, their
viewers watch the service work. That is the whole strategy.

## Keeping score

Four numbers, checked on day 30 against day 1:

| Metric | Where from |
| --- | --- |
| Orders and revenue | Store admin |
| New registered users | Store admin |
| Kaicash deposits | Wallet report — the clearest signal that retention is working |
| Click-throughs per streamer | Affiliate links |

Video views are not on that list on purpose. A leak video with 200k views and no orders
means the CTA failed, and the view count will hide that if you let it.
